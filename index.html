GS SWAM 2026!!!

This is the registration form for the inaugural Graduate Student SouthWest Applied Mathematics (GS SWAM) Conference, hosted at the University of New Mexico in Albuquerque, NM and organized by the UNM SIAM student chapter. We invite all graduate students in applied math and related programs from across the southwest (Arizona, Nevada and New Mexico) to register and participate as a speaker, poster presenter, or audience. Faculty and other professional staff are also welcome, though all presentations are to be done by students. Registration for all interested parties is free.

Conference Dates

The conference will formally begin Saturday, October 31st at 8 AM and end Sunday, November 1st at 1 PM. Informal social gatherings will be organized for the nights of October 30th and October 31st for all those who wish to attend. Halloween costumes are encouraged (spooky!).

Deadlines

This registration form will remain open through the conference. Abstract submissions are due by September 11th at 5 PM MST. Abstracts may be submitted up to two weeks after, though priority will be given to those submitted by September 11th. Abstracts and titles may be edited by the beginning of the conference as long as the nature of the talk remains mostly intact.

The UNM SIAM Student Chapter

As previously mentioned, this is a conference organized and funded by the UNM SIAM student chapter. As such, we have very limited funds and cannot offer any travel funds. This is also our first time hosting a conference... so while we believe we've got this in the bag, we welcome any participation or advice from the other student chapters in the southwest section of SIAM in organizing this event. Please note you do not need to be a member of SIAM to attend this event, though you are encouraged to join!

Please refer any general questions about the conference to our chapter vice president, Umair Ali Wani, at ualiwani@unm.edu.
